## 0.1.1

## 0.1.0
- First version: a tile of three volume keys (quieter, mute, louder), each running the Home Assistant action you give
  it. Across a tile as wide as it is high or wider they stand as minus, mute, plus; down a taller tile louder is at the
  top. Keys without an action are left out. A media player that is muted lights the mute key. From one cell up to 3x3.
